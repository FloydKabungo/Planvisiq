A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Clear from
the start.

Does every employee need a separate subscription?+

The proposed model is one company subscription with individual employee accounts. The company owner manages the plan and team access. Final pricing and user limits are still to be decided.

How will my team sign in?+

Employees will use their organisation’s workspace address and their own work email and password. The company owner or an admin will invite them; shared company passwords are not needed.

Can I try it before creating an organisation?+

Yes. Open the demo to explore a fictional shutdown project, upload a spreadsheet, customise charts and export reports. Demo records are saved only in your browser.

What is available in this version?+

This is a working frontend with spreadsheet imports, local dashboards and report exports. Company registration and sign-in are interactive previews. Live AI, secure accounts, email invitations, billing and shared cloud storage will follow with the backend.
